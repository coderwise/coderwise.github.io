Kotlin Multiplatform consulting

We help product teams ship Android and iOS from one Kotlin codebase — sharing the logic that should be shared and keeping the UI native where it matters. The work is done hands-on by the people you talk to, not handed to a rotating team.

KMP readiness assessment

A short, fixed-price review of your Android and iOS apps: what can move to a shared Kotlin core, what should stay native, and what it costs and risks. You get a clear go/no-go and a migration plan.

Typically 1–2 weeks

Migration and product delivery

Fixed-scope, incremental delivery. We extract business logic, networking and storage into shared Kotlin modules, keep the UI native or adopt Compose Multiplatform where it fits, and ship each step to production. Or we build a new multiplatform product from scratch.

Typically 3–6 months

Fractional KMP architect

Ongoing design authority and code review for your team: architecture and module boundaries, build and CI setup, and mentoring engineers who are new to Kotlin Multiplatform.

A couple of days a week
  • Our own apps run on Kotlin Multiplatform, with releases on Google Play and the App Store.

  • Production experience across consumer and financial Android apps used by millions of people.
  • One accountable point of contact, from the first assessment to the last release.

About

Coderwise Inc. is an independent software company based in Canada. We build mobile apps with a simple philosophy: do one thing well, respect the user's data, and feel native everywhere.

Our apps share a single Kotlin Multiplatform foundation — including our open-source libraries published on Maven Central as com.coderwise.libs — so features and fixes land on Android and iOS together.
