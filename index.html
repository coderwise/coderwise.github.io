About

Coderwise Inc. is an independent software company based in Oakville, Ontario, Canada. We build mobile apps with a simple philosophy: do one thing well, respect the user's data, and feel native everywhere.

Our apps share a single Kotlin Multiplatform foundation — including our open-source libraries published on Maven Central as com.coderwise.libs — so features and fixes land on Android and iOS together.
