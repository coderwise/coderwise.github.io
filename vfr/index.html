What the first version covers

Offline sectionals

Current FAA VFR charts stored on the device, so the map works with no signal. A clear warning when a chart cycle has expired.

Moving map

Your position, track and groundspeed over the chart, with an airport directory one tap away.

Weather and NOTAMs

METARs and TAFs on the map and on each airport page, plus NOTAMs for your route.

Simple routes

Direct-to and multi-leg routes with distance and time, and a track log of every flight.

ADS-B In

Traffic and in-flight weather from any GDL90 receiver, including Stratux, Sentry and Stratus.

Phones and cheap tablets

Designed for the Android hardware pilots really use as a kneeboard, not only the newest flagship.

What it is not

  • VFR only. No approach plates or IFR procedures.
  • United States charts only at launch.
  • Not available yet. It gets built if enough pilots want it, which is what this waitlist measures.

Why we are building it

ForeFlight does not run on Android. The apps that do are either free and rough, or treat Android as a second platform. Student and private pilots who fly with an Android phone or tablet deserve an app made for it.

ChartsOn comes from Coderwise, the company behind MapsOn, an offline maps app for Android and iOS. The map engine is the part we have already shipped.

Tell us what you fly with and what would make you switch: info@coderwise.com. Every reply is read.

ChartsOn will be an aid to situational awareness for VFR flight. It will not be certified for navigation and does not replace current official charts, a preflight briefing or pilot judgment.
